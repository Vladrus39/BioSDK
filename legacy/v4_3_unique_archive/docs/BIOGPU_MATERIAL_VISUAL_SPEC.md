# BioGPU Material Visual Specification

## Intended first real material

**2D lab-grown neuronal culture on MEA/HD-MEA**.

## Visual description

### Macro view

The system should look like a compact bio-electronic module:

- a clear cartridge or dish;
- visible fluid medium inside;
- a chip with a metallic-looking electrode grid at the bottom;
- cables or a docking interface leading to external acquisition/stimulation electronics;
- an outer enclosure or stage that maintains a stable environment.

### Micro view

Under microscope/magnification, the biological substrate should look like:

- branching neuronal cell bodies and neurites;
- a spread network over the electrode field;
- a living mesh-like structure rather than a rigid circuit board;
- local clusters and connecting neurite paths.

## System interpretation

What appears in the dish/cartridge is not a “fake neural rendering”, but the living substrate itself. The engineered part is the chip, chamber, controller and software stack.
