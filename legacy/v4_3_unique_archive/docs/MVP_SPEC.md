# MVP Specification v0.1

## Goal

Build a runnable software-defined BioGPU emulator with SimulatedMEA.

## Definition of Done

- End-to-end benchmark runs with one command.
- Results include metrics, baselines and reports.
- Interfaces are ready for future RealMEAAdapter.
- Data is saved in JSON/HDF5 and prepared for NWB-compatible export.

## What v0.1 does not prove

- It does not prove a living culture is better than a GPU.
- It does not perform wetlab work.
- It does not give stimulation parameters for biological tissue.
