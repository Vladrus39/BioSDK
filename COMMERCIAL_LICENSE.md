## 5. Why Open Core

The NSI-1.0 standard must be vendor-neutral and universally adopted. MIT
ensures zero friction for researchers, labs, and hardware vendors.

Enterprise features address production needs: safety-certified closed-loop
stimulation, HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, guaranteed uptime. These are
not needed for research and are not part of the standard.
